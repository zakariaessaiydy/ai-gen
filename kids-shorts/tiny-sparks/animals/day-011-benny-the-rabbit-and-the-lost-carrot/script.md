Facts (true):
1. Rabbits can move/turn each ear separately to listen in different directions. (RSPCA; PDSA rabbit care)
2. A rabbit's diet is mostly grass and hay; carrots are high in sugar and only an occasional treat. (RSPCA; PDSA; RWAF)
3. A happy rabbit's jump-and-twist in the air is called a "binky". (RWAF; PDSA)
Thumping the back feet is a real rabbit warning signal. (RSPCA)
