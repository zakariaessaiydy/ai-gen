Fact sources (kept simple + true):
1. Penguins are flightless birds; their wings are stiff flippers for swimming (National Geographic Kids, "Penguin").
2. Penguins "toboggan" — slide on their bellies over ice and snow (Australian Antarctic Division).
3. Emperor penguin fathers incubate the egg on their feet under a fold of skin (brood pouch) (British Antarctic Survey).
Note: "penguins waddle because their legs are short" — short legs set close together = the waddle (simplified, true).
