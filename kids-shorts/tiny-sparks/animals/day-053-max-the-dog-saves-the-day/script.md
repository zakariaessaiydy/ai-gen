# Day 53 — Max the Dog Saves the Day (LONG 16:9, 5 min 03 s)

Niche: 🦁 Animal Stories #8 · ages 3–5 · value: friendship ("Friends look out for each other") · cast: narrator,
Max the puppy (dog rig, red scarf), Bella the bunny, Kiki the kitten (cat rig), Super Bobo · places: meadow + forest
(painted) · energy layer standard, slow gaps.

GUESS WHO (a nose sniffs out of a bush, a tail wags behind it, think timer) → Max hops out → MEET Bobo (picnic!) →
SOUND "woof" + a puppy's "yip yip" (your turn) → 3 TRUE FACTS (a super nose — scent swirls from the picnic basket · a
fast, wiggly tail often means happy · puppies are born with their eyes closed — three sleepy puppies) → doggy tricks
(sit · paw · spin — your turn) → STORY: Bella counts her friends — Kiki is missing (worried, not scary) → "Who has a
super nose?" (think timer) → Max follows Kiki's paw prints: past the flowers (sniff with Max), he doesn't stop for a
ball, ACHOO at a flower, past the pond, to a hollow log → Kiki was playing hide and seek and fell asleep → found! →
"Max saved the day!" → friends look out for each other + "stay close to your grown-up" → picnic with a doggy treat →
remember (5 questions) → chant "Sniff, sniff! Wag, wag! Max saves the day!" → bye.

Fact sources: dogs' sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than ours (AKC, "How dogs smell") · tail
wagging carries many messages; a loose, fast, wiggly wag often signals a happy dog (simplified with "often") · puppies
are born with eyes closed and open them at about two weeks (AKC puppy development).
