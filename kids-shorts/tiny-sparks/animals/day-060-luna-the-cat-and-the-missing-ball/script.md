# Day 60 — Luna the Cat and the Missing Ball (LONG 16:9, 5 min 01 s)

Niche: 🦁 Animal Stories #9 · ages 3–5 · cast: Luna the kitten (new, grey cat with a purple bow) + Super Bobo + Max
the puppy (from Day 53) + a frog cameo + narrator · places: meadow + forest (painted) · energy layer standard.

GUESS WHO (pointy ears + whiskers over a bush, a curling tail, think timer) → Luna hops out → meet Bobo + her red ball
with a bell inside → SOUNDS: meow + purr (your turn) → 3 TRUE FACTS: super ears that turn to listen · whiskers help cats
feel things, even in the dark · cats sleep a lot (often more than half the day) → kitty play: stretch · pounce · chase
her tail (your turn: stretch) → STORY (value: giving back + playing together): Bobo kicks the ball too hard → it rolls
into the forest → "How can Luna find it? It has a bell!" (think timer) → Luna listens for the jingle: tiptoe past the
flowers (your turn) · past the pond (a frog — ribbit — not the ball!) · louder and louder to a hollow log → Max kept the
ball safe → gives it back → "play together?" → catch with three friends → cat nap → LET'S REMEMBER (5 questions) →
chant "Listen, listen! Jingle, jingle! Luna finds the ball!" → bye. Safety line: ask a grown-up before looking for a lost toy.
