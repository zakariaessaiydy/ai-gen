Fact sources (kept simple + true):
1. Most owls are nocturnal (active at night, rest by day) — a few species hunt by day, hence "most".
2. Owls can turn their heads about 270° — "almost all the way around" (never "all the way").
3. Owl flight feathers have soft, comb-like edges that muffle sound — owls fly almost silently.
