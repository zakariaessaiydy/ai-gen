Facts (true):
1. A rabbit's teeth grow continuously throughout its life; chewing grass and hay wears them down. (RSPCA; PDSA rabbit care; RWAF)
2. Rabbits' eyes sit high on the sides of the head, giving almost 360° vision (only small blind spots right in front of the nose and just behind the head). (RWAF; Britannica "Rabbit")
3. Wild European rabbits live in underground burrows — a network of tunnels called a warren. (The Mammal Society; Britannica)
Bonus (owl): many owls are nocturnal and sleep by day; their large eyes are adapted to see in low light. (RSPB; Britannica "Owl")
