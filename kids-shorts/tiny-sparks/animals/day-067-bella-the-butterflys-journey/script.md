Facts (simple, true): butterflies go through complete metamorphosis (egg → larva/caterpillar → pupa/chrysalis → adult);
they have taste receptors on their feet (tarsi) to "taste" leaves and flowers; adults drink nectar through a coiled,
straw-like proboscis. Butterflies avoid flying in rain and shelter under leaves. Sources: Smithsonian "Butterflies",
Natural History Museum (London) "How do butterflies taste?", Britannica "Lepidopteran".
