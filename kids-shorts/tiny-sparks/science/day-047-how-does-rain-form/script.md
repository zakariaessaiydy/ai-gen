Fact sources (kept simple + true):
1. The water cycle: evaporation → condensation into cloud droplets → droplets collide and merge until heavy enough to
   fall as precipitation → runoff/rivers back to the sea (NASA Climate Kids "What Is the Water Cycle?", Met Office).
2. Raindrop shape: small drops are nearly spherical; larger drops flatten on the bottom like a hamburger bun (USGS Water
   Science School, "The Shape of a Raindrop").
3. Rainbows: sunlight is refracted and reflected inside raindrops (Met Office "How is a rainbow formed?").
4. "The water in your bath was in a cloud long ago": Earth's water is recycled through the water cycle (USGS).
Experiment safety: warm (not boiling) water, poured BY A GROWN-UP; no tasting; said on screen + in the description.
