**Sources:** NOAA / NWS JetStream "Thunder" (thunder is the sound caused by lightning: the air in the channel is heated
extremely fast — hotter than the surface of the Sun — and expands rapidly, making the sound wave); NWS Lightning Safety
("When thunder roars, go indoors"); National Geographic Kids (light travels much faster than sound, so we see the flash
before we hear the thunder; nearby thunder is a sharp crack, distant thunder a low rumble).
