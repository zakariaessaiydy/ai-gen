Facts (true, simplified, not falsified):
1. Sunlight looks white but contains all the colours of the rainbow. (NASA Space Place — "Why Is the Sky Blue?")
2. Blue light is scattered by the gases/particles in the air more than other colours (shorter waves), so it
   reaches our eyes from all over the sky. (NASA Space Place)
3. On Mars, sunsets look blue — fine dust scatters blue light near the Sun's direction. (NASA/JPL, Curiosity & Spirit images)
