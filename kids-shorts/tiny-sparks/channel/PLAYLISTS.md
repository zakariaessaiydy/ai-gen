# Tiny Sparks — playlists (title + description). Every playlist: Made for Kids · Public · sort "Date added (oldest)".

## 1. Tiny Sparks Shorts — All Episodes
Every Tiny Sparks cartoon in one place! 🌟 Join Mila, Leo, Bobo the bear and Prof. Hoot for gentle, happy little videos that teach kindness, numbers, words, animals, science, puzzles and songs. A new episode every day — press play and learn along! Perfect for ages 3–7.

## 2. Kids Stories — Learn Kindness & Good Habits
Short story adventures where Mila, Leo and Bobo learn something new — sharing, saying please and thank you, washing hands, being brave and more. Each story teaches one easy lesson in 3 simple steps that little ones can copy at home. 💛 Ages 3–6.

## 3. Learn Numbers & Math
Count, add and play with numbers! 🔢 Bobo and friends count apples, balloons, stars and animals, then try fun first math — more or less, shapes, adding and taking away. Clear, slow and repeated so toddlers and preschoolers can count along. Ages 2–6.

## 4. English & French Words for Kids
Learn first words in English AND French! 🇬🇧🇫🇷 Colors, animals, food, body parts and more — each word is shown with a picture, said three times, then said in French, with a mini quiz to practice. Great for bilingual families and little language learners. Ages 2–7.

## 5. Animal Stories for Kids
Meet the animals! 🦁🐸🐼 Hear their sounds, discover where they live and what they eat, and enjoy a tiny story with each new animal friend. True animal facts made simple for curious little explorers. Ages 2–7.

## 6. Science for Kids
Why is the sky blue? Where does rain come from? 🚀 Prof. Hoot answers big kid questions with simple pictures and safe experiments to try with a grown-up. Science made easy, fun and true for curious minds. Ages 4–8.

## 7. Brain Games & Puzzles for Kids
Can you solve it before Leo? 🧩 Odd one out, spot the difference, patterns, shadows, mazes and riddles — every puzzle has a thinking timer so kids can guess before the answer. Builds attention, logic and memory. Ages 3–8.

## 8. Kids Songs & Sing-Alongs
Sing, dance and learn! 🎵 Original Tiny Sparks songs about counting, colors, the alphabet, washing hands, the planets and more — with a bouncing ball so everyone can sing along. Ages 2–6.

## 9. Moral Stories for Kids
Gentle stories with a big heart 📚 — honesty, sharing, patience, kindness and never giving up. Every story ends with one simple lesson and a question to talk about together. Calm enough for bedtime. Ages 3–8.
