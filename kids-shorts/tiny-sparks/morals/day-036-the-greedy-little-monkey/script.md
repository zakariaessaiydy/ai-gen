Retelling of the public-domain fable "the boy and the filberts" (Aesop) in our own words:
WANT (Momo finds a jar of nuts: "I want them ALL!") → WRONG CHOICE ("No! Mine, mine, mine!" — Bella and Bobo feel
sad; "How would YOU feel?" think timer) → he grabs a huge handful and his fist is STUCK in the jar (pull, pull ·
boing, boing, boing) → FEELING: upset, a bit sorry + the one word GREEDY ("wanting everything just for yourself") →
"Can you help Momo?" → Bobo: let go of some, take just a few · Bella: "If you hold too many, you can't get any!" →
POP, free → KIND CHOICE "I'm sorry I was greedy. Let's share!" → the nuts fly onto 3 leaf plates (one for Bella, one
for Bobo, one for me ×3; the child counts along) → "3 each!" → picnic (carrots + honey + nuts) → what else can we
share? (toys, crayons, hugs) → MORAL "Take a little, share a lot!" → LET'S REMEMBER (3 story cards + 4 think-timer
questions incl. "What do we say when we make a mistake? I'm sorry!") → YOUR TURN: share 6 bananas between 3 friends
(2 each) → chant ×3 → bye + "What can YOU share with a friend today?"
Nobody is bad, no punishment: the consequence is emotional + the stuck hand is silly, and it's repaired.
