superstar!" → 3 SHAPE RIDDLES (3 points · round like a ball · we draw it to say I love you) → say all our shapes
(circle, square, triangle, star, heart — your turn) → bye.

QA: one correct answer per board, readable at phone size; answers only after the timer; ✗ on Leo's pick, ✓ on the odd
one; the super-puzzle grid has exactly one shape without a twin.
