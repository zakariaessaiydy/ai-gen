12 puzzles, easy → harder: 1 an 8 among 3s · 2 a 1 among 5s · 3 a six-dot die among fours (count the dots) · 4 a group
of 3 apples among groups of 2 · 5 a green 7 among red 7s · 6 a 6 among 9s ("they look alike, but they are different") →
stretch (jump ×2, clap ×3) → 7 five stars among fours · 8 a 2 among 10s · 9 "which one is not four?" (four dots, four
fingers, the numeral 4… and five dots) · 10 a basket with 1 ball among 3s → draw a number 1 in the air → 11 the
upside-down 7 → SUPER PUZZLE: every number has a twin but one (the 9) → number superstar → 3 riddles (after 1 before 3 ·
fingers on one hand · after 9) → count to ten together (your turn) → bye.
