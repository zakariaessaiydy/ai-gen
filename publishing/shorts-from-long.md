## Upload settings (every Short)

- Upload the vertical file as is: YouTube makes it a Short automatically (vertical, under 3 minutes).
- Audience: **Yes, it's made for kids**.
- Visibility: **Public**, or scheduled for a date in the next few days (not weeks away).
- Playlist: the one named in the table (the same playlist as the long video).
- Post 1-2 Shorts a day rather than all at once, so each one gets its own test audience.
- Description: the line below + `Watch the full episode on the Tiny Sparks channel!` + `#kids #kidslearning #preschool #toddlers #shorts`.
