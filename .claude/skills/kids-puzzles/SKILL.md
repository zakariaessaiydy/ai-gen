# kids-puzzles — your turn to think!

Read `.claude/skills/make-kids/SKILL.md` first.

## The loop (one puzzle ≈ 10–15 s)

**SHOW → ASK → THINK (timer) → LEO TRIES (wrong) → HINT → REVEAL → YAY**

1. **Show** the puzzle big and clean (stage-space SVG drawn from kit props / sets / Critters).
2. **Ask** in ≤ 8 words: "Which one is different?" (Prof. Hoot, `point`).
3. **Think**: `ThinkTimer` 3–5 s, characters look at the camera, quiet tick (sfx
   `clock-tick-soft`), NO talking — the child's turn.
4. **Leo tries** (first 2 puzzles of a video): a confident wrong answer → AnswerMark ✗ +
   a gentle "Hmm, not quite!" — kids LOVE knowing better than Leo.
5. **Hint** (optional for harder ones): a sparkle or a zoom on the right area.
6. **Reveal**: the correct item pulses/zooms, AnswerMark ✓, and WHY in one line ("It's the only
   one that can fly!").
7. **Yay**: Confetti + a point on a score strip (Mila 1 · Leo 0 — a light running game).

Difficulty ramps: easy → easy → medium → medium → harder → a final "super puzzle".

## Puzzle types (all buildable from the kit)

Odd one out (3 fruit + 1 sock) · What comes next (colour/shape patterns: 🔴🔵🔴🔵❓) · Shadow
match (Critter filled with KINK at 40% as the shadow) · Spot the difference (two copies of a set,
3 changes, circle them one by one) · Which is bigger/longer/more · Count & compare · Memory (show
4 cards 3 s, flip, "where was the bunny?") · Simple maze (thick path, a Critter walks it after the
timer) · Riddles ("I'm yellow and I shine in the sky…") · Sorting (fruit vs toys).

## Rules

- ONE correct answer, unambiguous at phone size (no "well, technically…").
- Timer ≥ 3 s for ages 3–5, 5 s for older; never skip the pause.
- No talking during the timer except a soft "Hmm…".
- Never humiliate a wrong answer — Leo's mistakes are funny for HIM too ("Silly me!").
- Spot-the-difference: differences must be BIG (colour or object, not 3 pixels).

## Formats

Short (9:16, 30–60 s): 2–3 puzzles ("Only geniuses get #3 🧠" style hook, but honest). Long
(16:9, MIN 5 min, 5–10 min): 10–15 puzzles with a running score and a final super puzzle.
