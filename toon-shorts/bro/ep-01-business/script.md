# EP 1 — Bro thought he could get rich in ONE day

**Series:** BRO THINKS HE'S HIM · **Length:** 39.4s · **Cast:** Bro, Dee, Customer (extra)
**v2 script.** v1 had one predictable joke stretched over 30s; v2 has a punchline about
every 3–4 seconds.

| # | on screen | line | why it's funny |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Bro close-up, smug, hands on hips | **BRO:** I quit my job today. | confident claim in second 1 |
| 1 | cut wide, Dee unimpressed | **DEE:** You don't have a job. | instant deflation |
| 2 | Bro, finger guns, head tilt | **BRO:** That's how fast I quit. | doubles down instead of admitting it (the series' engine) |
| 3 | punch-in + shake, sparkles | **BRO:** Trust me. I got this. | catchphrase #1 |
| 4 | dutch angle, god rays, pointing up | **BRO:** I'll be a millionaire by tonight. | stakes as high as possible |
| 5 | SMASH CUT: ONE bottle, WATER $10 (WATER $1 poster planted behind) | **BRO:** Phase one. | the gap between the epic claim and the tiny reality |
| 6 | customer walks in | **CUSTOMER:** Ten dollars? For water? | the audience's own reaction, said out loud |
| 7 | Bro presents the bottle | **BRO:** Sir, this is premium water. | |
| 8 | customer, side-eye | **CUSTOMER:** What makes it premium? | setup... |
| 9 | Bro close-up, proud, beat of silence first | **BRO:** ...The price. | ...punchline, circular logic |
| 10 | customer points → camera pans to WATER $1 | **CUSTOMER:** It's one dollar right there. | the planted gag pays off |
| 11 | Bro FLEXES, god rays, chain sparkles | **BRO:** Yeah, but do they have ME? | main-character delusion |
| 12 | customer leaves | **CUSTOMER:** That's why it's one dollar. | the burn: the hardest laugh |
| 13 | THE STARE | **BRO:** ...Hater. | one-word cope, very quotable |

## Why it should spread
- **A joke every 3–4s** keeps viewers from swiping away.
- **Three quotable lines:** "...The price.", "...Hater.", "So I'm up nine." People repeat them
  in the comments.
- **The ending invites arguments**, like "technically he's not wrong", and comment activity
  pushes the video to more people.
- **The planted poster** rewards a rewatch, and rewatches help it loop.
