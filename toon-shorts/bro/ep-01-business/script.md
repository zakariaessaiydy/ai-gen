# EP 1 — Bro thought he could get rich in ONE day

**Series:** BRO THINKS HE'S HIM · **Length:** 30s · **Cast:** Bro, Dee, Customer (extra)

**Hook (0–2.4s):** frame 0 is Bro in close-up, smug, hands on hips, title bar already up.
A big confident statement in the first second.

| time | on screen | line |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | Bro close-up, room, smug, hands on hips | **BRO:** I'm done working for other people. |
| 2.5 | wide two-shot, Dee arms crossed | **DEE:** Bro. What's the plan? |
| 4.0 | punch-in + shake, finger guns, sparkles | **BRO:** Trust me. I got this. *(catchphrase #1)* |
| 6.4 | dutch angle, god rays, pointing up | **BRO:** I'm starting a business. |
| 8.4 | SMASH CUT: street, table, ONE bottle, sign WATER $10. MINI MART poster behind says WATER $1 | **BRO:** Entrepreneurship. |
| 10.3 | customer walks in | |

| 20.5 | card: 3 HOURS LATER | |

## Why it should work
- **Plant → payoff:** the WATER $1 poster is visible behind him from the first street frame;
  sharp viewers spot it before the customer says it, and that's what drives comments.
- **Escalation in three steps:** a $10 price, then the store's $1, then he drinks his own stock.
- **The signature ending** is identical in every episode, so the series is recognizable.

## Production notes
- Voices: Bro = confident and fast, Dee = flat and tired, Customer = gruff and short.
- Speed: "Ten dollars." is said slowly and proudly; "...Market research." starts after a
  half-beat pause.
