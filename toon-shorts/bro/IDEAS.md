last one visually before it's said), and a **cope line** he says straight to camera. Titles
always start with "Bro…" because the title bar is the hook.

## Recurring gags to grow (callbacks build fans)
- The **Mini Mart poster** always undercuts his plan (WATER $1, GYM 50% OFF, ...).
- **Dee's sip/stare reaction** before the big line.
- A **running counter** in the corner over episodes: "BRO'S NET WORTH: $0.37".
- Each episode's cope line can end up on merch later ("Market research.").

# Other series for this same kit (ready when Bro is established)

The toon kit is series-agnostic: a new series = a new `series/<name>.ts` cast file + a
`toon-shorts/<name>/` folder. Ranked by viral potential:

1. **The Overthinker.** He gets "ok" as a text and the screen splits between REALITY and HIS BRAIN
   (50 disasters). Very relatable, huge comment bait ("this is me").
2. **The World's Worst Detective.** Trench coat and magnifying glass, investigating "who moved my
   chair 2 cm" like an FBI case. Always wrong; an evidence-board gag every episode.
3. **The Guy Who Can't Lie.** "Do you like my haircut?" "...Honestly?" Simple format, endless
   episodes, very shareable.
4. **The Broke Millionaire.** Billionaire talk next to a bank balance of $2.37. Overlaps with Bro,
   so it could become a Bro spin-off instead.
5. **The Uncle With Ancient Wisdom.** Confident, terrible advice told to the camera; it fails
   immediately on screen.
6. **The Unluckiest Guy Alive.** He only tries to do normal things; the universe says no. Almost
   no dialogue, so it works in any language.
